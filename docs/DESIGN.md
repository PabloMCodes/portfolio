# Design Direction

## Desired Feeling

The portfolio should feel like a premium, interactive engineering and product website—not a generic student developer portfolio.

## Principles

- Be visually ambitious but structurally restrained.
- Use strong typography and excellent spacing.
- Give project media a high-quality presentation.
- Use meaningful animation.
- Include interactive engineering demonstrations.
- Explain systems through architecture and system diagrams.
- Show subtle personality.
- Deliver excellent responsiveness.

Animation should help communicate engineering concepts.

## Avoid

- Skill percentage bars
- Giant logo clouds
- Random particle effects
- Pointless gradients
- Fake terminals without purpose
- Unnecessary 3D
- Effects disconnected from Pablo's work
- Excessive animation

Do not choose permanent fonts, colors, or detailed design tokens yet. Those decisions will be made iteratively.
